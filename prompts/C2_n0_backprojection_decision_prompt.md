CONTEXT: `config.R:456` sets `N0_BACKPROJECT <- TRUE`, so per-cell respiration is
`K / (N_inoc * exp(r*delta))`, i.e.

    log R  =  log K  -  log N_inoc  -  r*delta

Respiration is an explicit DECREASING function of the fitted growth rate. `delta` is
`fit_start_time`, the O2 peak, which `config.R:451` itself describes as "largely lag-phase". Median
`r*delta` = 0.68 (deflation ~2x, max 36x), and `exp(r*delta)` peaks at ~2.7x near 32-34 C - i.e. the
deflation is LARGEST at the growth optimum, which mechanically flattens the respiration TPC.
Recomputing Arrhenius slopes from `derived_N0_R_results_with_carbon.csv` with and without the term:

    E_R shipped   I 0.52  II 0.42  III 0.40  IV 0.30  para 0.34   (ordered; IV lowest)
    E_R no-backproj I 0.63  II 0.55  III 0.59  IV 0.61  para 0.55   (flat)

So the entire between-taxon ordering in respiratory thermal sensitivity - and with it Clade IV as the
cheap-fever extreme, Fig 1c/1d, Fig 2 and Fig 4d - may be generated by a term built from `r`. The two
settings are NOT a right/wrong pair: TRUE assumes ZERO lag (exponential growth at rate `r` from
inoculation), FALSE assumes PURE lag (no growth at all before the window). Both are wrong; the truth
is intermediate. Compounding this, the repo contradicts itself: `config.R:446-456` says TRUE "injects
the inverted growth curve and fit noise into respiration -> no thermal optimum, noisy", while
`04_trim_selector.R:247-252` says "this comment used to blame the N0 back-projection ... that was
wrong ... Do NOT set N0_BACKPROJECT <- FALSE". Neither cites an analysis. C2 produces the analysis
that should have settled it.
