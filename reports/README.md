## Where the numbers live

`tools/` holds every script that produced a number in `REPRODUCTION.md` or
`N0_SENSITIVITY.md`, together with its JSON or CSV output — `headline_C1.json`,
`compare_tables.json`, `known_inconsistencies.json`, `c2_arms.json` and the rest.
`C11_scale_free/` is self-contained: `R/` computes, `tables/` and `figures/` are
its outputs, and the report renders from them.

## Superseded conclusions {#superseded}

Each report stands as the record of what was found **at the time**. Where a
conclusion has since been overtaken, a dated note has been added at the head of
the affected section rather than the text being rewritten — the audit trail is
the point. As of 2026-08-11 that applies to:

* **`REPRODUCTION.md` §on the etc-GEM.** The etc-GEM half did not reproduce
  (capacity regenerating 1.33–1.37× higher, an anchor change, a temperature grid
  the code could not emit). **That section is now historical: the etc-GEM has
  been cut from the manuscript.** The code, the submodule and the vendored
  outputs remain in the repository.
* **`N0_SENSITIVITY.md`.** Predates the in-code `N_inoc` regeneration and the
  C11 scale-free result. The reconciliation it left open has since closed, and
  the headline CUE claim no longer depends on the N₀ treatment at all.
